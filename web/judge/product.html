In a configured workspace

From “they paid” to “here’s why” in four steps

About the private workspace This is how a check runs in a configured CrashCart workspace. None of it runs on this public site.

  1. 01

    Make a test purchase

    Start a checkout in PayPal’s test environment and approve it yourself. No real card is charged.

  2. 02

    Confirm the payment yourself

    After you approve it on PayPal, you confirm the payment with a click. CrashCart never completes a charge on its own.

  3. 03

    Check the delivery promise

    CrashCart asks PayPal and your store directly, then applies the same rule every time: one ticket, within 60 seconds.

  4. 04

    Let the assistant dig in

    If something’s off, an AI assistant works through the records in the background. It suggests where to look, but the payment and ticket records decide the result. You can stop it at any time.
