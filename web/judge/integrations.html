The tools, and their jobs

Built around the answer.

Start with the recorded case, or look at what’s available in a separately configured workspace.

P

Payment records

PayPal Sandbox

Recorded in demo

Confirm the money arrived, even when the store lost the reply. A checkout success message alone isn’t proof of payment.

What it does +

The private test workspace creates and captures Orders v2 payments, reads payment status directly, and verifies payment notifications before trusting them.

The public demo shows saved payment records. It never starts a checkout or contacts PayPal.

To run a new checkout: use your own PayPal Sandbox app and test buyer. Test money only.

Inspect the recorded payment ↗
D

Investigation assistant

DeepSeek on Modal

Recorded in demo

Choose which payment and ticket records to inspect next, then explain the findings with citations.

What it does +

The assistant runs in the private workspace. Its saved 8 November investigation is available in the demo, separately from the 22 October repair comparison.

It proposes where to look. Fixed code checks whether the delivery promise was kept; the assistant cannot override that result.

Reading the saved investigation makes no new AI request.

Follow the saved investigation ↗
B

Delivery timing

Bryntum Community Scheduler

Optional view

Put payment and delivery on the same timeline, and see which tickets missed their promised time.

Where to see it +

The actual MIT-licensed Community Scheduler shows the recorded repair comparison here and appears in the video at 2:10–2:20.

The delivery-timing page needs no account or service key. The simpler timeline inside the investigation remains a separate view. Open delivery timing.

Watch the timing view ↗
A

Linked-record exploration

AG Studio

Optional workspace

Explore linked payment and ticket records in five evidence panels, and ask questions about saved cases.

Availability and access +

The optional workspace uses AG Studio 3.0.0 and its Agent Framework. It is implemented and tested locally, but is not deployed on this public site.

A permitted AG Studio licence is required. This optional workspace is not part of the public submission experience. This integrations page is not AG Studio output.

Read the workspace guide ↗
K

Checkout recordings

KERNEL

Owner-enabled

Review what the customer experienced alongside the payment and delivery records.

What it records +

KERNEL provides a remote browser for approved test checkouts and recordings in the private workspace.

A new session needs the workspace owner’s access and authorization. The public demo starts no remote browser session.

P

Repeatable checks

Postman / Newman

Workspace tools

Check the payment and delivery records, their links, and the files exported from a case.

How it is used +

Executable API and linked-record checks run in a separately configured private workspace.

These checks are separate from browsing the recorded example. Downloading its files makes no payment or AI request.

S

Store connections

Shopify & WooCommerce

In preview

Order and fulfilment connectors are being prepared. They haven’t been verified with a real merchant store yet.

What’s available today +

Store installation and connecting your own store aren’t available on this public demo. It uses a workshop test store.

The connectors have local signed-event tests, but no real merchant store is connected yet.
