    Questions
    
      
    Good to know
    
      
    
        
    
          Does this page take a payment or contact PayPal?
          
    No. You’re exploring a recorded test order. Nothing here charges money, contacts PayPal, connects a store or starts an AI investigation.
    
        
    
        
    
          What exactly was recorded?
          
    A PayPal Sandbox test order for a $29 workshop ticket, due within 60 seconds of payment. The store lost the payment reply and never issued the ticket. The recovered order shows the repaired checkout using that same recorded payment, without making a new purchase.
    
        
    
        
    
          Is the AI assistant running here?
          
    No. The assistant section shows an earlier saved investigation of a different order. Reading it makes no AI request. Payment and delivery records, rather than the assistant’s opinion, determine the answer.
    
        
    
        
    
          Which stores does CrashCart work with today?
          
    The current example uses a workshop test store. Shopify and WooCommerce connections are still in preview and haven’t been tested with a real store. There’s no store installation or connection available here yet.
    
        
    
        
    
          How would I check my own orders?
          
    The private workspace can run test checkouts after its owner sets up PayPal Sandbox access. It isn’t a self-service connection for your shop yet. This recorded example needs no sign-in.
    
        
    
        
    
          What can I download?
          
    Take the order details for both outcomes and the saved checkout data. The ZIP also lets you run the same comparison on your computer without making a payment. Downloading a file doesn’t run it.
