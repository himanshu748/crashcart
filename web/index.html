A few useful answers.
The essentials, before you get started.


Does CrashCart make real-money payments?
CrashCart uses PayPal test payments with a workshop test store. No real money changes hands. Starting an investigation does not make a payment; a new test checkout needs buyer approval.


What is the difference between a replay and a new checkout?
A replay reruns supported behavior from saved checkout inputs without making another payment. A new checkout creates a new test payment. CrashCart labels saved evidence and new tests separately.


Does the assistant decide whether a payment succeeded?
The assistant proposes investigation steps and explains what the evidence supports. Fixed checks use the payment and store records to decide whether the delivery promise was kept, broken or still needs evidence.


Can I connect any store?
This version supports one registered, owned workshop store and its approved test scenarios. It does not scan arbitrary shops or make production repairs.


How do I get into a workspace?
Use the private access key supplied by your workspace owner. Your saved investigations, records and available recordings live inside that workspace.
